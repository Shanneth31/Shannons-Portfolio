About Me

Creative Mind, Design Obsessed

I've been making things for as long as I can remember, apparently drawing Doctor Who characters for my Nan was the early warning sign.

These days, I'm a Graphic Designer with a slightly unhealthy enthusiasm for turning ideas into things people can actually see, touch or interact with. From packaging and POS to campaigns, events and 3D concepts, I like getting stuck into the whole process, figuring out the idea, solving the awkward bits and making the final thing look good.

I'm naturally curious, annoyingly detail oriented and always up for learning something new. Give me a weird brief, a tight deadline or a problem that needs untangling and I'm probably already opening Illustrator.

Creative? Obviously.
Organised? Surprisingly.
Obsessed with making things better? Absolutely.

The part where I accidentally became a jack of all trades.

Digital Art gave me a bit of a creative playground — 3D modelling, animation, coding, web design, graphic design, photography, videography and filmmaking were all part of the degree. Instead of specialising in one discipline, I learned how different creative skills could work together, which has stuck with me ever since.

For my dissertation, I decided a normal project wasn't quite enough and built a 7-level platform game from scratch. I 3D modelled and animated it, created the sound effects and designed a colour-based mechanic where the player's D-pad colours changed the difficulty of their surroundings, taking inspiration from Hue and Mario. Each level gradually ramped up the challenge.

Turns out learning a little bit of everything is pretty useful when you're a designer who likes doing everything.
