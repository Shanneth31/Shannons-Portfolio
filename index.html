InContrast

Graphic Designer · May 2025 – present
Scan me to see the bunny in AR

My main jobs at InContrast include designing and conceptualising print ready files and doing any studio work that comes into our inbox varying from digital to print work.

Prepress I'm responsible for prepress preparation across a wide range of cardboard POS. This involves checking bleed, colour accuracy in CMYK, image resolution, and file linking, ensuring all elements align correctly with cutter lines.
3D + Conceptual Development Alongside prepress, I've developed skills in Rhino 8, producing industry-standard 3D models of POS structures. I conceptualise high-impact display solutions and help bring "wow factor" campaigns to life.
Studio Inc Working with the team, I help manage a shared client inbox, scheduling and coordinating projects including 2D POS, digital marketing and externally printed materials — balancing multiple deadlines and client expectations.
POS Print Design We artwork a wide range of cardboard POS, from FSDUs to smaller 2D items like barkers and wobblers. Working from a single campaign concept, I adapt artwork across multiple cutter guides for consistency and precision.

Freelance

Ongoing · Personal Projects

During my free time I enjoy doing some freelance work. I like being versatile so I have created different types of work for different customers including wedding stationery, business cards, photo edits and personalised prints.

Wedding Stationery Wedding invitations are exciting to create — customers want something that shouts "we're getting married" but also want it personal. I ask couples about their interests so I can create a truly personal invitation.
Business Cards A recognisable brand has a pinnacle logo that needs to be plastered everywhere! I love word and picture play — creating a logo that emphasises the brand's identity in a fun, memorable way.
Photo Edits Photo manipulation is great fun — from levitating portraits to popping a dinosaur into wedding photos. A lot of my work has been for cosplayers wanting a relevant background to their costume.
Personalised Prints There's always a spot in the house that could do with a print — from wedding bouquets to whimsical bathroom posters, starting from pen and paper then cleaning up in Illustrator.
